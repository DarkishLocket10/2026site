Selection notes for the four saved booklet pages:

- Because the Internet: Red track-list booklet back, the only Booklet scan.
- Trench: Left (back-cover) panel of the outer booklet spread. Source marked Watermark because the opposite front panel has a white patch; selected back panel has no visible watermark.
- So Close to What: Copyright-bearing star-overlay photo selected as likely booklet back; pagination is not independently verified. The separately signed front and orange reverse were rejected as a bonus signed insert. Original approximately 600 pixels; no upscaling.
- Imaginal Disk: Last supplied booklet page, The Ballad of Matt & Mica. This is a gatefold CD, not a jewel case. Original 600x593; no upscaling.

Inspection and provenance: every final saved artwork image was visually inspected, using contact sheets and individual views; disc orientation and one spine edge were corrected after inspection. Dimensions, JSON paths, circle alpha, and hashes were checked. Crop coordinates and per-asset URLs are in _research/processing.json; all shell request URLs and outcomes are in _research/requests.jsonl. Selected CAA inventories remain under music/_research.

Shell downloads used User-Agent yash-portfolio/1.0 (https://yashnilay.ca), cached responses, a 1.1-second per-host interval, and exponential backoff on 429/5xx. Web search was also used for discovery. No artwork was generated, painted, or reconstructed. Edits were crops, perspective/ellipse correction, resizing, and alpha masking.
